# Support Policy

1. Refunds are issued in full for duplicate charges within 30 days of the original transaction.
2. Refunds for change-of-mind cancellations are issued at 50% within 14 days of purchase, and not issued after 14 days.
3. Billing disputes must be resolved within 5 business days of being reported.
4. Technical issues that prevent core product usage are treated as high priority.
5. Account lockouts due to failed login attempts are resolved by identity verification, not by support override.
6. Shipping delays of more than 7 business days beyond the estimated delivery date qualify for a shipping credit.
7. Subscription cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing cycle; no partial-month refunds are issued for cancellations.
8. Customers on the Enterprise plan receive priority support with a 4-hour response SLA.
9. Data export requests must be fulfilled within 10 business days per data portability policy.
10. Support cannot process refunds for third-party marketplace purchases; those are redirected to the marketplace.
